How to spend a study block

A repeatable loop that fits a night-shift week — no marathon sessions required:

  • First 25 min — read one subtopic in the Topic Library (patho → assessment → nursing priorities).
  • Next 15 min — take a short quiz on that system. Missed questions are the real study material.
  • Last 10 min — write your weak-area note on the topic page and check off what you covered.
